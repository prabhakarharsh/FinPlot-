Our core privacy & accuracy guarantees:

  • Zero Credentials: We never ask for bank credentials, card numbers, OTPs, or bank account logins. Data is loaded strictly via CSV upload or sample data.
  • Deterministic Arithmetic: All totals, summaries, and scenario timelines are calculated by pure JavaScript formulas. The AI never guesses numbers.
  • Neutral Review Flags: Unusual activity is flagged for your review with neutral prompts. It is never labeled as fraud.
